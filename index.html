    Paste & Classify Pool Plasma

    Same sheet layout as Section 2, just different headers: paste the pooled-plasma columns for one or more runs. The run header (row 1, e.g. “July 3A 2026 MRM 1.1 …”) picks the batch, and the “Pooled Human Plasma BRH…_R01 Calc'd Conc.” label (row 2) becomes the Sample ID. Mean/CV and blank spacer columns are dropped — the app recalculates those. Replicates pasted under the same run header are paired for you.
